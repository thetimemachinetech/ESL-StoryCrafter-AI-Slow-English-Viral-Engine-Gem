## 🚀 Quickstart Guide

1. **Click the Gem URL:** Open the [ESL StoryCrafter AI Gem](https://gemini.google.com/gem/1MDyCjZXmPg-YkWOs65AGsnPNSZvBJ-nN?usp=sharing) in your Google Gemini account.
2. **Type "Generate":** The AI will automatically analyze current global ESL trends and pitch 3 high-retention story concepts tailored for your publishing window.
3. **Select & Render:** Reply with your chosen concept number to generate the multi-scene master text-to-video prompt. Copy and paste this prompt directly into your preferred AI video generation tool (like Luma, Runway, Kling, or SeaArt).
4. **Get Your Metadata:** Reply "NEXT" to instantly receive your YouTube tags, platform-specific captions, and thumbnail concepts.
